Part 2 · After AI, ML & DL

NLP, Generative AI, LLMs, AI Agents & Agentic AI

These five words get used as if they mean the same thing. They don't, because they aren't even the same kind of thing:

NLP = a field GenAI = a capability LLM = a model AI agent = a system Agentic AI = a way of working

Tap any shape on the map to see where it sits.

Where are agents? They aren't a circle on this map. Agents are systems built on top of these models. See “How they stack” below.

Step 4 · Inside an AI agent

The agent loop: think → act → observe → repeat

An LLM on its own answers once and stops. An agent runs in a loop: it decides on a step, uses a tool, looks at the result, and decides again, until the task is done.

The 4 ingredients of an agent

  • Brain: an LLM that reasons and decides
  • Tools: APIs, search, code, browser, email
  • Memory: what it has done and learned so far
  • Loop: keeps going until the goal is met

Take one away…

  • No tools → it's just a chatbot (talks, can't do)
  • No loop → a single tool call, not an agent
  • No LLM → a fixed automation script (if X then Y)
  • No memory → it forgets earlier steps and repeats itself
Step 5 · The most confused pair

AI agent vs Agentic AI

An AI agent is a thing, one worker. Agentic AI describes how much independence a system has. It's usually several agents, planning and adapting toward a bigger goal with little hand-holding.

AI agentAgentic AI
What it isA single software workerA design approach / system with high autonomy, often many agents
ScopeOne well-defined task (“book the cheapest train”)An open-ended goal or workflow (“run my Goa trip end to end”)
Who starts itUsually you, for each taskCan start itself on a schedule or a trigger (a cancellation, a price drop)
PlanningFollows a loop for the task in front of itBreaks the goal into sub-tasks, assigns them, re-plans when things change
Agents involvedUsually oneOften several specialists plus a coordinator
MemoryMostly within the taskLonger-term: remembers preferences and past outcomes
Human roleApproves or reviews the resultSets the goal and limits; steps in only for exceptions
ExampleSupport bot that looks up your order and issues a refundSupply-chain system that watches stock, forecasts demand, reorders from suppliers and escalates only problems
Honest note: these terms are still loosely used in industry, and many people say “agentic AI” to mean any AI agent. It's best understood as a spectrum of autonomy, not a hard line.

Step 7 · How to identify

The 4-question test

Think of any AI product or feature and answer honestly. You'll land on the most specific label.

The rule of thumb
  1. Does it DO things in the world (call APIs, book, send, click, run code)?
    → Yes: it's an agent. Go to Q4.
  2. Does it CREATE something new (text, image, audio, code)?
    → Yes: it's GenAI. Go to Q3.
    → No, but it works on language: NLP.
  3. Is it text from a large language model?
    → Yes: LLM. No (pictures, music, video): GenAI.
  4. Does it plan, re-plan and pursue a bigger goal on its own, often with several agents?
    → Yes: Agentic AI. No, one task you hand it: AI agent.
Quick verbs: understands · creates · writes · does · manages
